Our History

Founded in 2005 by Santosh Bhagat, Shubh Labh Construction began as a small team of passionate engineers with a vision to transform Nepal's infrastructure landscape. Starting with just 5 employees, we've grown into a respected construction firm with over 150 skilled professionals.

Our journey has been marked by significant milestones - from our first municipal project in Biratnagar to large-scale infrastructure developments across Province 1. We've built lasting relationships with clients through our commitment to quality, safety, and innovation.

Today, Shubh Labh Construction stands as a symbol of excellence in Nepal's construction industry, with a portfolio of over 200 successfully completed projects and a reputation for delivering beyond expectations.
