Building Excellence Since 2005

Shubh Labh Construction was founded in 2005 by a team of visionary engineers and construction experts led by Santosh Bhagat. With over 18 years of experience in the construction industry, we have grown to become one of the most trusted names in Biratnagar and throughout Nepal.

Our journey began with a small team of 5 dedicated professionals who shared a common vision: to transform Nepal's infrastructure landscape through innovative engineering and uncompromising quality. Today, we employ over 150 skilled workers and have completed more than 200 projects across various sectors.
